# Daily Low Breakout Master Report (H4)

## FLFTC Research Engine

### Breakout Phase – Study #4

---

# Objective

The goal of this study was to determine whether breaking the previous day's low creates bearish continuation.

Question:

> When a market breaks below yesterday's low, does momentum continue lower?

This study is the bearish counterpart to the Daily High Breakout study.

---

# Markets Tested

- EURUSD
- GBPUSD
- USDCHF
- AUDUSD
- NZDUSD
- USDCAD
- USDJPY
- Gold (XAUUSD)
- SP500
- NAS100

---

# Methodology

## Bearish Daily Low Breakout

- Current Low < Previous Day Low
- Current Close < Previous Day Low

Only the first breakout signal per day was counted.

Evaluation Horizons:

- 1 Candle
- 3 Candles
- 6 Candles

# Major Discoveries

## Discovery #1

Bullish breakouts outperform bearish breakouts.

Across nearly every market:

- Daily High Breakouts were stronger.
- Daily Low Breakouts were weaker.

---

## Discovery #2

Indices strongly resist downside continuation.

SP500:

- 44.49%

NAS100:

- 50.21%

This suggests downside breakouts frequently reverse.

---

## Discovery #3

Gold maintains a strong bullish bias.

Daily High Breakout:

- 52.45%

Daily Low Breakout:

- 46.11%

Gold clearly prefers upside continuation.

---

## Discovery #4

The bearish breakout concept is weak overall.

Unlike London Breakouts and New York Breakouts, Daily Low Breakouts failed to produce a strong scalable edge.
