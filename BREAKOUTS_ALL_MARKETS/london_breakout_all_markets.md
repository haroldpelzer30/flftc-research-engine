# Objective

The goal of this study was to determine whether breaking the London Session range creates a measurable continuation edge.

This concept is one of the most commonly traded breakout strategies in Forex and Index markets.

Question:

> After price breaks the London Session range, does momentum continue in the breakout direction?

---

# Markets Tested

- EURUSD
- GBPUSD
- USDCHF
- AUDUSD
- NZDUSD
- USDCAD
- USDJPY
- Gold (XAUUSD)
- SP500
- NAS100

---

# Methodology

## Bullish London Breakout

- Price breaks above London Session High
- Candle closes above London High

## Bearish London Breakout

- Price breaks below London Session Low
- Candle closes below London Low

Only the first breakout signal per direction per day was counted.

Evaluation Horizons:

- 1 Candle
- 3 Candles
- 6 Candles
