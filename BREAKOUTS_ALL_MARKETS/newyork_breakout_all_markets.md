# New York Breakout Master Report (H4)

## FLFTC Research Engine

### Breakout Phase – Study #2

---

# Objective

The goal of this study was to determine whether breaking the New York Session range creates continuation momentum.

Question:

> After price breaks the New York Session range, does momentum continue in the breakout direction?

This study serves as a direct comparison against the London Breakout study.

---

# Markets Tested

- EURUSD
- GBPUSD
- USDCHF
- AUDUSD
- NZDUSD
- USDCAD
- USDJPY
- Gold (XAUUSD)
- SP500
- NAS100

---

# Methodology

## Bullish New York Breakout

- Price breaks above New York Session High
- Candle closes above New York High

## Bearish New York Breakout

- Price breaks below New York Session Low
- Candle closes below New York Low

Only the first breakout signal per direction per day was counted.

Evaluation Horizons:

- 1 Candle
- 3 Candles
- 6 Candles

# Major Discoveries

## Discovery #1

London Breakouts outperform New York Breakouts.

Across nearly every market:

- London produced stronger continuation.
- New York produced weaker continuation.

---

## Discovery #2

Forex breakouts are largely ineffective.

Most Forex pairs remained below:

- 50%
- 51%

even after breakouts.

---

## Discovery #3

Indices continue dominating.

The strongest markets remain:

- SP500
- NAS100

regardless of methodology.

---

## Discovery #4

Breakouts appear highly market-dependent.

The concept works on:

- NAS100
- SP500

It does not appear to work reliably on most Forex pairs.
