## FLFTC Research Engine

---

# Objective

The goal of this study was to determine whether breaking the previous day's high creates bullish continuation.

Question:

> When a market breaks above yesterday's high, does momentum continue higher?

This study serves as the continuation counterpart to the Daily Sweep study.

---

# Markets Tested

- EURUSD
- GBPUSD
- USDCHF
- AUDUSD
- NZDUSD
- USDCAD
- USDJPY
- Gold (XAUUSD)
- SP500
- NAS100

---

# Methodology

## Bullish Daily High Breakout

- Current High > Previous Day High
- Current Close > Previous Day High

Only the first breakout signal per day was counted.

Evaluation Horizons:

- 1 Candle
- 3 Candles
- 6 Candles

# Updated Breakout Conclusion

After three breakout studies, a clear pattern is emerging:

> Not all breakouts are equal.

The strongest breakout opportunities appear to come from:

1. Session Breakouts (London/New York)
2. Equity Index Markets
3. Markets with existing trend persistence

The weakest breakout opportunities remain traditional Forex pairs.

The market continues to matter more than the setup.
