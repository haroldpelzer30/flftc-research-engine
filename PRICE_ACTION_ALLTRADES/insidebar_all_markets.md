# Inside Bar Research Report (H4)

## Study Overview

This study tested Inside Bar patterns across:

* EURUSD
* GBPUSD
* USDCHF
* AUDUSD
* NZDUSD
* USDCAD
* USDJPY
* Gold (XAUUSD)
* SP500
* NAS100

Methodology:

### Bullish Inside Bar

* Current candle fully contained within previous candle
* Bullish close

### Bearish Inside Bar

* Current candle fully contained within previous candle
* Bearish close

Evaluation Horizons:

* 1 Candle
* 3 Candles
* 6 Candles

# Price Action Phase Conclusions

### Observation #1

The same three markets dominated every study:

* NAS100
* SP500
* Gold

---

### Observation #2

Forex never produced a truly elite result.

Despite tens of thousands of signals tested, most Forex pairs remained trapped around:

* 50%
* 53%

---

### Observation #3

Price Action worked best when aligned with bullish market drift.

The strongest markets were:

* NAS100
* SP500
* Gold

All three possess long-term bullish tendencies.

---

### Observation #4

The pattern mattered less than the market.

Five different price-action concepts produced the same winners.

That is unlikely to be a coincidence.

---

# Final Verdict

Grade: B+

Price Action produced several useful findings, but none surpassed the strongest Momentum research.

The biggest lesson of the phase:

> Different patterns kept producing the same winners.

Which means the real edge may not be the pattern itself.

The real edge appears to be understanding which markets reward pullbacks and continuation.
