# Engulfing Candle Research Report (H4)

## Study Overview

This study tested Bullish and Bearish Engulfing Candles across:

* EURUSD
* GBPUSD
* USDCHF
* AUDUSD
* NZDUSD
* USDCAD
* USDJPY
* Gold (XAUUSD)
* SP500
* NAS100

Methodology:

### Bullish Engulfing

* Previous candle bearish
* Current candle bullish
* Current candle body completely engulfs previous candle body

### Bearish Engulfing

* Previous candle bullish
* Current candle bearish
* Current candle body completely engulfs previous candle body

Evaluation Horizons:

* 1 Candle
* 3 Candles
* 6 Candles

---

# Overall Findings

## Biggest Takeaway

This was probably the most surprising study of the entire Price Action phase.

Why?

* 49%

* Gold remained strong
* SP500 remained strong
* NAS100 remained strong

The same markets keep winning.

# Research Conclusions

### Observation #1

Engulfing Candles underperformed expectations.

This is one of the most widely taught patterns in retail trading.

Yet the results were generally weaker than:

* Daily Sweeps
* Pin Bars
* Double Bottoms

---

### Observation #2

The same three markets keep winning.

Every major Price Action study has been dominated by:

* NAS100
* SP500
* Gold

---

### Observation #3

Forex continues to struggle.

The majority of Forex pairs remain trapped around:

* 50%
* 52%

regardless of pattern.

---

### Observation #4

The pattern matters less than the market.

This is now the fourth consecutive Price Action study supporting the same conclusion.
