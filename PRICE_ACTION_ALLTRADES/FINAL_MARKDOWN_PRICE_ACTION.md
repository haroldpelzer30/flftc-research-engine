## FLFTC Research Engine

### Phase 3: Price Action

# Objective

The goal of the Price Action phase was to determine whether raw market structure and candlestick patterns contain measurable predictive value.

Unlike:

- Momentum Studies (RSI)
- Volatility Studies (Bollinger Bands)

Price Action focuses entirely on:

- Market structure
- Candlestick behavior
- Liquidity events
- Reversal patterns

The central question:

> Does price itself contain a tradable edge?

# Studies Completed

## 1. Daily Sweep

### Methodology

Bullish Sweep:

- Current low breaks previous low
- Candle closes back above previous low

Bearish Sweep:

- Current high breaks previous high
- Candle closes back below previous high

### Best Results

| Market | Result |
|----------|---------:|
| NAS100 | 58.04% |
| SP500 | 55.53% |
| Gold | 55.13% |

### Key Finding

One of the strongest studies of the entire project.

Produced:

- Large sample sizes
- Strong consistency
- Excellent scalability

---

## 2. Double Bottom / Double Top

### Methodology

Bullish:

- Consecutive lows at similar levels

Bearish:

- Consecutive highs at similar levels

### Best Results

| Market | Result |
|----------|---------:|
| NAS100 | 56.35% |
| SP500 | 56.20% |
| Gold | 54.55% |

### Key Finding

Produced strong results but generally underperformed Daily Sweeps.

---

## 3. Pin Bars

### Methodology

Bullish Pin Bar:

- Long lower wick
- Small candle body

Bearish Pin Bar:

- Long upper wick
- Small candle body

### Best Results

| Market | Result |
|----------|---------:|
| NAS100 | 57.55% |
| SP500 | 55.37% |
| Gold | 53.11% |

### Key Finding

One of the strongest studies completed.

Especially effective on equity indices.

---

## 4. Engulfing Candles

### Methodology

Bullish:

- Bullish candle fully engulfs previous bearish body

Bearish:

- Bearish candle fully engulfs previous bullish body

### Best Results

| Market | Result |
|----------|---------:|
| NAS100 | 55.15% |
| SP500 | 54.95% |
| Gold | 53.70% |

### Key Finding

Widely taught pattern.

Results were weaker than expected.

Underperformed:

- Daily Sweeps
- Pin Bars
- Double Bottoms

---

## 5. Inside Bars

### Methodology

Bullish:

- Current candle contained within previous candle
- Bullish close

Bearish:

- Current candle contained within previous candle
- Bearish close

### Best Results

| Market | Result |
|----------|---------:|
| NAS100 | 57.31% |
| SP500 | 54.27% |
| Gold | 53.30% |

### Key Finding

Inside Bars produced some of the strongest Forex results of the phase.

Still dominated by NAS100.

- Daily Sweep
- Pin Bar
- Inside Bar
- Double Bottom
- Engulfing

All pointed toward:

- NAS100
- SP500
- Gold

The strongest performers:

- NAS100
- SP500
- Gold

all exhibit long-term bullish tendencies.

# Master Research Conclusion So Far

Across Momentum, Volatility, and Price Action, a consistent theme emerged:

> Strong markets reward pullbacks, reversals, and continuation patterns more effectively than weak or range-bound markets.

The evidence increasingly suggests that:

- Market selection matters more than indicator selection.
- Trend persistence matters more than pattern complexity.
- Robust edges come from identifying markets with favorable structural behavior.

This conclusion would later be reinforced by the Trend Following phase.
