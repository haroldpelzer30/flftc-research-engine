# Pin Bar Research Report (H4)

## Study Overview

This study tested Pin Bar (Wick Rejection) patterns across:

* EURUSD
* GBPUSD
* USDCHF
* AUDUSD
* NZDUSD
* USDCAD
* USDJPY
* Gold (XAUUSD)
* SP500
* NAS100

Methodology:

### Bullish Pin Bar

* Long lower wick
* Small candle body
* Lower wick at least 2x body size

### Bearish Pin Bar

* Long upper wick
* Small candle body
* Upper wick at least 2x body size

Evaluation Horizons:

* 1 Candle
* 3 Candles
* 6 Candles

---

# Overall Findings

## Biggest Takeaway

Pin Bars produced the clearest separation between:

* Forex
* Gold
* Indices

of any Price Action study so far.

Forex was mostly noise.

Indices dominated.

Gold once again ranked near the top.
