# Daily Sweep Research Report (H4)

## Study Overview

This study tested Daily High/Low Liquidity Sweeps across:

* EURUSD
* GBPUSD
* USDCHF
* AUDUSD
* NZDUSD
* USDCAD
* USDJPY
* Gold (XAUUSD)
* SP500
* NAS100

Methodology:

* Bullish Sweep = Price breaks previous daily low and closes back above it.
* Bearish Sweep = Price breaks previous daily high and closes back below it.
* Evaluation Horizons:

  * 1 Candle
  * 3 Candles
  * 6 Candles

---

# Overall Findings

## Biggest Takeaway

Daily Sweeps generate a massive number of opportunities, but most Forex markets produced only a small statistical edge.

The strongest results appeared in:

1. NAS100
2. SP500
3. Gold (XAUUSD)

This suggests that Daily Sweeps may be more effective in markets with a long-term bullish drift than in mean-reverting currency markets.

# Research Conclusions

### Observation #1

Daily Sweeps generate enormous sample sizes.

Several markets produced over:

* 8,000 signals
* 9,000 signals

making this one of the most statistically robust studies performed so far.

---

### Observation #2

Forex markets showed only a minor advantage.

Most Forex pairs clustered between:

* 50%
* 52%

which is barely above random.

---

### Observation #3

Indices clearly outperformed Forex.

NAS100:

* 58.04%

SP500:

* 55.53%

Both significantly exceeded most Forex results.

---

### Observation #4

Gold behaved more like an index than a currency pair.

Gold produced:

* 55.13%

with over 5,000 signals.

---

# Final Verdict

Grade: B-

Daily Sweeps are not a powerful standalone Forex edge.

However, they appear to have meaningful value in:

* NAS100
* SP500
* Gold

The concept may work best when combined with market structure, trend, or momentum filters.
