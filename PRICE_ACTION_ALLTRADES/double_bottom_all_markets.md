# Double Bottom / Double Top Research Report (H4)

## Study Overview

This study tested Double Bottom and Double Top formations across:

* EURUSD
* GBPUSD
* USDCHF
* AUDUSD
* NZDUSD
* USDCAD
* USDJPY
* Gold (XAUUSD)
* SP500
* NAS100

Methodology:

* Double Bottom = Price revisits a recent low within a 0.15% threshold.
* Double Top = Price revisits a recent high within a 0.15% threshold.
* Lookback Window: 10 Bars
* Evaluation Horizons:

  * 1 Candle
  * 3 Candles
  * 6 Candles

---

# Overall Findings

## Biggest Takeaway

Double Bottoms and Double Tops generated massive sample sizes across nearly every market.

However, the results were surprisingly similar to Daily Sweeps:

* Forex pairs mostly clustered around 50-52%.
* Gold produced stronger bullish continuation.
* Equity indices again outperformed Forex.

The pattern itself appears to matter less than the market in which it occurs.

# Research Conclusions

### Observation #1

Double Bottoms produced enormous sample sizes.

Several markets generated:

* 10,000+
* 15,000+

signals.

This makes the results statistically meaningful.

---

### Observation #2

Indices once again dominated Forex.

NAS100 and SP500 finished first and second.

This is now becoming a recurring theme throughout the project.

---

### Observation #3

Gold continues behaving differently from Forex.

Gold consistently ranks near the top regardless of methodology.

---

### Observation #4

Double Bottoms did not beat Daily Sweeps.

The strongest Daily Sweep result:

* NAS100 = 58.04%

The strongest Double Bottom result:

* NAS100 = 56.35%

Daily Sweeps remain the Price Action leader.

---

# Final Verdict

Grade: B

Double Bottoms are legitimate but not exceptional.

The pattern appears strongest when combined with naturally bullish markets such as:

* NAS100
* SP500
* Gold

The results reinforce the project's most important lesson:

> The market matters more than the pattern.
