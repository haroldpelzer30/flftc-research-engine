# Objective

The goal of the Trend Following phase was to determine whether markets exhibit measurable directional persistence after a trend has already been established.

Unlike:

- Momentum Studies (RSI)
- Volatility Studies (Bollinger Bands)
- Price Action Studies (Sweeps, Pin Bars, Engulfings)

Trend Following asks a different question:

> Once a market starts moving in a direction, does it continue moving in that direction?

# Studies Completed

## 1. EMA50 / SMA50 Cross

Classic moving-average crossover.

### Question

> Does a short-term trend crossover create an edge?

### Best Results

| Market | Result |
|----------|---------:|
| SP500 | 70.31% |
| NAS100 | 60.87% |
| Gold | 55.21% |

### Key Finding

Strong win rates appeared but signal counts were small.

Example:

- SP500 = 64 signals
- NAS100 = 69 signals

Interesting but limited.

---

## 2. EMA50 / EMA200 Cross

Classic Golden Cross / Death Cross.

### Question

> Do major trend shifts create an edge?

### Best Results

| Market | Result |
|----------|---------:|
| NAS100 | 64.29% |
| USDCHF | 60.53% |
| AUDUSD | 58.70% |

## 3. EMA20 Pullback

Trend + Pullback model.

| NAS100 | 56.31% |

## 4. EMA200 Trend Filter

Pure trend test.

### Question

> Is simply being above the 200 EMA enough?

### Best Results

| Market | Result | Signals |
|----------|---------:|---------:|
| NAS100 | 56.74% | 3,715 |
| SP500 | 56.41% | 3,726 |
| Gold | 52.80% | 19,054 |

### Key Finding

This became one of the most important studies in the entire project.

No entries.

No patterns.

No indicators beyond trend direction.

Trend alone produced an edge.

---

## 5. Higher High / Higher Low Structure

A major confirmation result.

# Major Discoveries

## Discovery #1

The market matters more than the indicator.

Across:

- RSI
- Bollinger Bands
- Daily Sweeps
- Pin Bars
- Engulfings
- EMA Filters
- Market Structure

the same markets repeatedly won.

---

## Discovery #2

Trend persistence appears real.

The strongest trend-following results were generated by:

- NAS100
- SP500
- Gold

regardless of methodology.

---

## Discovery #3

Indicators often measure the same thing.

EMA200 Filter and Higher High / Higher Low Structure produced nearly identical results.

This suggests:

> Indicators may simply be another way of measuring trend.

---

## Discovery #4

Trend beats complexity.

The strongest scalable edge wasn't:

- RSI
- Bollinger Bands
- Double Bottoms
- Engulfings

It was:

> Being in the direction of the dominant trend.
