# EMA50 / SMA50 Cross Research Report (H4)

## Study Overview

This study tested a classic Trend Following model across:

* EURUSD
* GBPUSD
* USDCHF
* AUDUSD
* NZDUSD
* USDCAD
* USDJPY
* Gold (XAUUSD)
* SP500
* NAS100

Methodology:

### Bullish Cross

* EMA50 crosses above SMA50

### Bearish Cross

* EMA50 crosses below SMA50

Evaluation Horizons:

* 1 Candle
* 3 Candles
* 6 Candles

---

# Overall Findings

## Biggest Takeaway

The first Trend Following study immediately produced some of the strongest results of the entire project.

Most importantly:

* Gold remained strong.
* NAS100 remained strong.
* SP500 exploded higher.

However, signal counts were much smaller than Price Action studies because moving average crosses occur less frequently.
