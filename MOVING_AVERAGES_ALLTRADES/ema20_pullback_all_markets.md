# EMA20 Pullback Research Report (H4)

## Study Overview

This study tested pullbacks to the EMA20 within an established EMA50 trend across:

* EURUSD
* GBPUSD
* USDCHF
* AUDUSD
* NZDUSD
* USDCAD
* USDJPY
* Gold (XAUUSD)
* SP500
* NAS100

Methodology:

### Bullish Pullback

* Price above EMA50
* Price pulls back near EMA20
* Bullish candle close

### Bearish Pullback

* Price below EMA50
* Price pulls back near EMA20
* Bearish candle close

Evaluation Horizons:

* 1 Candle
* 3 Candles
* 6 Candles
