# EMA200 Trend Filter Research Report (H4)

## Study Overview

This study tested a pure trend filter across:

* EURUSD
* GBPUSD
* USDCHF
* AUDUSD
* NZDUSD
* USDCAD
* USDJPY
* Gold (XAUUSD)
* SP500
* NAS100

Methodology:

### Bullish Condition

* Close > EMA200

### Bearish Condition

* Close < EMA200

No entry pattern.

No pullback.

No crossover.

No confirmation.

Simply:

> Is being above the 200 EMA enough?

Evaluation Horizons:

* 1 Candle
* 3 Candles
* 6 Candles
