# Higher High / Higher Low Research Report (H4)

## Study Overview

This study tested pure market structure across:

* EURUSD
* GBPUSD
* USDCHF
* AUDUSD
* NZDUSD
* USDCAD
* USDJPY
* Gold (XAUUSD)
* SP500
* NAS100

Methodology:

### Bullish Structure

* Current High > Previous High
* Current Low > Previous Low

(Higher High + Higher Low)

### Bearish Structure

* Current High < Previous High
* Current Low < Previous Low

(Lower High + Lower Low)

No indicators.

No moving averages.

No oscillators.

Pure price action.

Evaluation Horizons:

* 1 Candle
* 3 Candles
* 6 Candles
