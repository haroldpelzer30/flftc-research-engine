* EURUSD

* USDCHF

* NZDUSD
* USDCAD
* USDJPY
* Gold (XAUUSD)
* SP500
* NAS100

Methodology:

### Bullish Cross

* EMA50 crosses above EMA200

### Bearish Cross

* EMA50 crosses below EMA200

Evaluation Horizons:

* 1 Candle
* 3 Candles
* 6 Candles

---

# Overall Findings

## Biggest Takeaway

This study produced the smallest sample sizes of any research phase so far.

Why?

Because major trend changes are rare.

While some win rates appear impressive, many are based on:

* 10 signals
* 20 signals
* 30 signals

which makes statistical confidence much lower than previous studies.

# Comparison vs EMA50 / SMA50

## EMA50 / SMA50

Advantages:

* Much larger sample sizes
* More frequent signals
* Better statistical confidence

Example:

* NAS100 = 69 bullish signals
* SP500 = 64 bullish signals

---

## EMA50 / EMA200

Advantages:

* Stronger trend confirmation
* Higher win rates in some markets

Disadvantages:

* Tiny sample sizes

Example:

* NAS100 = 14 bullish signals
* SP500 = 11 bullish signals

---

# Research Conclusions

### Observation #1

Long-term trend changes are extremely rare.

Many markets generated:

* Fewer than 150 signals
* Sometimes fewer than 20 signals

over the entire database.

---

### Observation #2

The strongest win rates came from the smallest samples.

This is exactly why signal count matters.

A 64% win rate sounds amazing until you discover it came from only 14 signals.

---

### Observation #3

EMA50 / SMA50 appears more practical.

Although win rates were slightly lower:

* More opportunities
* Better statistical confidence
* Easier implementation

---

### Observation #4

This study did not dethrone the project leaders.

The strongest findings remain:

* Gold RSI + Daily Structure
* NAS100 Daily Sweep
* NAS100 Pin Bar
* SP500 EMA50/SMA50
